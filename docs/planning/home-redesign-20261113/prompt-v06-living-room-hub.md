## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept / revise a game home's circulation design.
Input image 1 is the ACTUAL EXISTING LIVING ROOM from the game. Its recognizable furniture and their relative arrangement are the fixed functional reference.
Input image 2 is a previous concept used ONLY for warm polished 3D rendering, tactile materials and two child scale figures. Its room arrangement is NOT the design to copy.

Create ONE finished concept image of a SIMPLE RECTANGULAR FAMILY HOME LIVING ROOM that is also the central connector to SIX other existing rooms. This replaces the rejected polygon-shaped independent entrance lobby.
The user's explicit design is: EXTERIOR ENTRANCE -> SMALL OPEN ENTRY ZONE -> EXISTING LIVING ROOM -> OTHER SIX ROOMS. There is no separate public hall and no extra corridor room. The home still has exactly SEVEN functional rooms total: this living room, bedroom, study, bathroom, kitchen, dining room, balcony.

SHAPE IS CRITICAL:
One regular rectangle as the complete shown room footprint. Straight continuous perimeter walls meeting at RIGHT ANGLES ONLY. Four square corners. No diagonal walls, no beveled perimeter, no octagonal or polygon lobby, no zigzags, no recessed alcove wings, no U-shaped foyer enclosure. Main entry is on the front edge and directly opens into the living room floor. The small shoe-changing entry zone is part of the living room, without a dividing wall or extra doorway.

PRESERVE THE CURRENT LIVING ROOM ARRANGEMENT FROM INPUT 1:
- small sofa with pink seat and teal frame at the rear-center facing toward the front;
- rectangular rounded oak tea/coffee table on the pink rug immediately in front of that sofa;
- tall two-door teal wardrobe to the LEFT of the seating group;
- wooden display/storage cabinet at the rear-left;
- teal armchair with cream rug in the rear-right reading corner;
- narrow wooden activity desk/table forward of that reading corner at the right;
- low bookcase toward the front-right;
- small basket/console table toward the front-left;
- windows and family wall pictures on the rear wall.
Keep these objects recognizable, keep their relative positions, and do not replace them with a completely different living-room furniture layout. You may improve the modeling finish and increase empty floor margins OUTSIDE the fixed furniture groups so the new side-wall doorway approaches are unobstructed. Do NOT move the seating into the middle of the main entry route. Do not shrink the furniture or characters to fake more space.

SIX EXISTING ROOM ENTRANCES:
Put exactly THREE normal-sized real doors into the straight LEFT side wall: 书房 toward the back, 卧室 near the middle, 浴室 toward the front.
Put exactly THREE entrances into the straight RIGHT side wall: 厨房 toward the back, 餐厅 near the middle, 阳台 toward the front (this one may be a glazed door).
These six closed doors connect directly from the living room's clear peripheral walking space into the six retained separate game scenes. Behind them the existing room models and furniture are unchanged, so do NOT draw or invent new bedroom, kitchen, dining or bathroom interiors. Do not put beds, stoves, dining tables or washing machines inside this living room. No additional door labeled 客厅: we are already INSIDE the living room.
Every side door is embedded in the connected rectangular perimeter wall with actual plaster above the lintel. No freestanding doors or isolated wall fragments.
The exterior FRONT ENTRANCE is distinct from these six room doors. Show a wide front threshold and a small coat-hook/shoe-bench entry area against the front-left edge, directly connected to the living-room floor. For the dollhouse view the front exterior wall may be omitted/cut away at the illustration boundary; do not put a detached tall door prop at the center of the floor. No internal vestibule box.

PLAYABILITY:
A broad uninterrupted strip of open floor from the front entry into the living room. Clear side circulation around the fixed furniture groups reaches all six doors. Main paths approximately 2.4 m wide in design intent. A generous open activity area in the front half of the living room. Exactly TWO normal-scale stylized child avatars in that open floor, a ponytailed girl in yellow and a child in blue, walking side by side, approximately 1.5 m compared with roughly 2.6 m full wall height. Plenty of empty ground visible around them. No additional people and no clutter in doorways. This is a home with usable seating and activities, not an empty door-selection lobby.

ART AND CAMERA:
Warm high-quality stylized 3D life-simulation game art, rounded bevels, soft oak grain, ivory walls with muted sage trim, restrained cloth weave, warm sunlight and contact shadows. Preserve the distinctive teal/pink existing furniture palette. Landscape full-room architectural cutaway. High-angle approximately 60 degrees down orthographic view from near the front center so the two straight parallel side walls and all SIX side entrances are understandable. Clear regular rectangular footprint on a thin slab. Light neutral backdrop. No floating floors, split levels, bridges or underfloor openings.

CONCEPT LABELS:
Small outside Chinese labels by the six relevant side doors, exactly once each: 书房, 卧室, 浴室, 厨房, 餐厅, 阳台. A small front label 入户玄关. Central unobtrusive label 客厅. Top title: 入户即客厅. Small subtitle: 方正布局 · 六间房内景沿用.
No other writing, game HUD, navigation arrows through the floor, watermark, plan inset, collage or panel divisions. One final rendered concept image.
